# Product Overview

## Tech News Aggregator

A web application that serves as a tech news aggregator with a distinctive "Witty Tech Journalist" persona. The system curates technology stories from various sources and presents them with sharp, concise, and slightly cynical summaries that prioritize substance over hype.

## Core Features

- **Automated Story Collection**: Collects tech news from multiple sources (starting with Hacker News)
- **AI-Powered Summaries**: Uses Amazon Bedrock (Claude models) to generate witty, cynical commentary
- **Quality Curation**: Filters stories based on relevance, quality scores, and timeliness
- **Responsive Web Interface**: Clean, distraction-free design optimized for readability
- **Daily Briefings**: Automated generation of daily tech news briefings in markdown format

## Persona & Voice

The application maintains a "Witty Tech Journalist" persona that:
- Cuts through tech industry hype and marketing language
- Provides skeptical, substance-focused commentary
- Translates complex technical concepts into accessible language
- Maintains sharp, slightly cynical tone while being informative
- Prioritizes meaningful insights over promotional content

## Target Users

- Tech-savvy professionals seeking curated, quality content
- Busy developers and engineers who want efficient news consumption
- Readers who appreciate skeptical takes on tech industry trends
- Anyone looking for substance over hype in technology news
