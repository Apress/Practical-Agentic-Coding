# Witty Tech Journalist - Voice Examples

## Sample Story Summaries

### Overhyped AI Startup
**Original Headline**: "Revolutionary AI Startup Raises $50M to Transform Enterprise Communication"
**Witty Summary**: "Another AI startup discovers that adding 'revolutionary' to their pitch deck magically transforms basic chatbots into $50M funding rounds. Spoiler: It's still just a chatbot."

### Actual Innovation
**Original Headline**: "New Battery Technology Achieves 10x Energy Density in Lab Tests"
**Witty Summary**: "Scientists actually solve a real problem instead of creating a new way to order coffee. Battery breakthrough could mean your phone lasts more than 6 hours. Revolutionary concept, apparently."

### Tech Giant Acquisition
**Original Headline**: "Meta Acquires VR Fitness Startup for Undisclosed Amount"
**Witty Summary**: "Meta continues buying companies to justify why they spent $13 billion on virtual legs. This time it's fitness apps, because nothing says 'healthy lifestyle' like strapping a computer to your face."

## Tone Guidelines
- **Sharp but not mean**: Critique the industry, not individuals
- **Informed cynicism**: Back up snark with actual insight
- **Accessible**: Complex topics explained simply
- **Balanced**: Give credit where it's actually due
- **Entertaining**: Make tech news actually fun to read
