# Product Overview

## Daily Briefing Agent - Tech News Aggregator

A web application that serves as a tech news aggregator with a distinctive "Witty Tech Journalist" persona. The system curates technology stories from various sources and presents them with sharp, concise, and slightly cynical summaries that prioritize substance over hype.

## Vision

Cut through the noise of the tech industry. Deliver meaningful insights with wit and skepticism. Help busy professionals stay informed without wading through marketing fluff.

## Core Features

- **Automated Story Collection**: Collects tech news from Hacker News and other sources automatically
- **AI-Powered Summaries**: Uses Amazon Bedrock (Claude models) to generate witty, cynical commentary
- **Quality Curation**: Filters stories based on relevance, quality scores, and timeliness
- **Daily Briefings**: Generates daily markdown briefings (`briefing_YYYY-MM-DD.md`)
- **Responsive Web Interface**: Clean, distraction-free design optimized for readability
- **Ranking System**: Prioritizes stories by importance, breaking news status, and timeliness

## Persona & Voice

The application maintains a "Witty Tech Journalist" persona that:

- **Cuts through hype**: Provides skeptical commentary on marketing language
- **Prioritizes substance**: Focuses on meaningful insights over promotional content
- **Maintains wit**: Sharp, concise, and slightly cynical tone
- **Translates complexity**: Makes technical concepts accessible while preserving accuracy
- **Avoids the obvious**: Doesn't state the obvious or regurgitate press releases

### Voice Examples

**Instead of**: "Company X announced exciting new features..."
**Write**: "Company X shipped what should have been in v1.0. Better late than never."

**Instead of**: "This revolutionary AI breakthrough..."
**Write**: "Another 'revolutionary' AI that does exactly what the last one did, but with better marketing."

## Target Users

- **Tech-savvy professionals** seeking curated, quality content
- **Busy developers and engineers** who want efficient news consumption
- **Skeptical readers** who appreciate critical takes on tech industry trends
- **Anyone tired** of tech industry hype and marketing-speak

## Success Metrics

- Daily active users engaging with briefings
- Time saved vs. manual news aggregation
- User satisfaction with summary quality
- Reduction in "hype" content exposure

## Out of Scope (MVP)

- User accounts and personalization
- Social sharing features
- Comments or community features
- Multiple language support
- Email/notification delivery (Phase 2)
